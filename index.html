Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator really free?

Yes. Create and download QR codes without a subscription, account, or email address.

What file format should I choose?

PNG and JPEG work well for screens and everyday sharing. SVG and EPS are vector formats that stay sharp when resized for print.

Is my link stored anywhere?

The QR code is generated in your browser. The link is not sent to an account or stored by this page.

Can I change the image size?

Yes. Choose 256, 512, 1024, or 2048 pixels for PNG and JPEG downloads.
